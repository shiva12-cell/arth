# Arth house style (every screen, every report)

- Rupees: Rs 12,34,567.89 - Indian digit grouping, two decimals. Lakh and crore in narrative text (Rs 12.3 lakh).
- Dates: 1 Oct 2026. Financial year written FY 2026-27.
- Percentages to two decimals: 12.34%.
- Every number that came from the internet shows its source and timestamp, e.g. "Yahoo Finance, 1 Oct 2026 15:30 IST, delayed".
- Plain English on every screen: Price, Holdings, Profit or loss, Maturity amount. No code, no raw data, no technical errors.
- Every calculator shows its formula in a small expandable "How this is calculated" section.
- Nothing on the platform is investment advice; every page footer says so in one line.
